Interactive diorama · Three.js + WebGL

Tidehaven

The island is cut open along two walls, aquarium‑style, so the surface, the animals and the sandy seabed all read at once. Slide the tide, raise a wind, run the day around, and let one of the rays show off.

Turn sound on — score, surf and breeze are generated live. Everything is procedural: no models were loaded.
