02 / ANNOTATION

Skeleton & sac

Six features worth marking before you put a bird on a frame. Lines are drawn to the specimen on the left, at scale 1.32, cadence running.

Field notes

01
The pouch is not a netThe gular sac is a scoop, not a cast — about eleven litres when brimmed, roughly a third of the bird's own weight. Fully loaded on an incline, expect visible sag and a lazier waddle.
02
Heel‑steered, toe‑drivenLegs do all the work: the pelican pedals with its toes, webbing spreading each stroke into a small damp paddle. Knees track forward, ankles stay quiet.
03
The other leg is invisibleOnly the near leg is drawn. The far leg is a translucent rumour behind the down tube — we include it for completeness and immediately bury it.
04
Neck as a damperThe S‑curve neck is a spring. Over cobbles it compresses, keeping the bill — and the fish inside it — almost level with the horizon.
05
Brakes: none reportedNo levers, no thumbs, no helmet. Descent strategy is loud and the beak is used as a horn. This is documented behaviour, not a joke.
