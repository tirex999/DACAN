“Took the corner on the drops at 06:41, pouch full of spray, and never once put a foot down. The gular pouch, distended, behaved as a low-pressure brake duct — I have seen nothing like it on the dune road.”

  • 1

    Gular pouch

    Ballast and brake duct. Deployed in crosswind; also a crate for sardines, keys, one ticket.

  • 2

    Primaries on the grips

    Wing tips wrapped to the bar. Steering is shoulder-led; braking is by tail-feather flare.

  • 3

    Saddle & pygostyle

    The bird sits on the saddle, not the other way round — a point worth recording.

  • 4

    Crank-driven tarsus

    Direct drive, webbed feet on level pedals. Short femur, long shank, knees forward.

  • 5

    28″ tubulars

    High pressure, sand-friendly. Rear tyre holds a fish tail through the casing, to this day.
