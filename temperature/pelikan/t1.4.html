Pelican riding a bicycle

Two metres of wing folded into a 56 cm frame. He does not steer with his wings — he steers with the pouch, which is roughly the size of a shopping bag and currently full of hot air.

Pedalling, legs and wheels below are all driven from one master clock, so the cadence you dial below genuinely turns the cranks, pumps the drumstick, spins the wheels — the gular wobble is free.
