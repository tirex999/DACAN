Field notes

Built for the coast road.

A pelican can carry eleven litres in its throat pouch — roughly three times its own stomach. It has simply never been asked to carry a backpack, a helmet lock, or a bicycle pump.

Specimen sheet

  • Wingspan to 2.7 m — the widest of any North American bird
  • Cruises at 45–50 km/h, and drafts in a V to save the flock
  • Throat pouch: 11–13 L, used as a net, not a lunchbox
  • Webbed feet: excellent rudders, mediocre on flat pedals
  • Illustrated here with a bell, a cap, and one fish in a basket
