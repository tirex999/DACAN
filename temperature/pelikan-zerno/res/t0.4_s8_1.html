Field notes from the feed zone

Plunge-diving is a descent discipline

10–20 m

Brown pelicans hunt by folding the wings and dropping from that height, hitting the water head-first and scooping fish in the gular pouch. Stage 14 has a cobbled descent. The bird considers this an advantage.

0.4 s

Time to drain the pouch before the swallow. Water is not lunch. Drain, then swallow — a lesson in pacing that every domestique should tattoo on the frame.

V-formation

Pelicans ride thermals in loose formation and can glide for kilometres with barely a wingbeat. Drafting is not cheating; it is ornithology. The breakaway has noticed.
