Plate VII  ·  Pelicanus velocipedus  ·  38°N 0°12′W

A pelican, observed riding bicycle.

Six seasons on the headland road, and the verdict holds: it pedals with fully extended webbed feet, steers with a wing, and keeps the day's catch in a pouch lashed to the top tube. Ballast, they tell us, is what keeps a bird upright at speed.
