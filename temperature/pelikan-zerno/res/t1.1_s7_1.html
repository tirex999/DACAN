Field notes

Every bird, on every bicycle, in every cartoon, since the 1890s. It is the shortest joke in the English language and the longest-running contest in American letters.

Here it is as a proper coastal plate: the saddle set high, the legs absurdly long, the gular sac catching the headwind like a spinnaker. The bird is not skilled. The bird is committed.

Notice the wing on the handlebar — a pelican has no hands, so it steers with enthusiasm. The chain is drawn, not welded. The fish in the pannier is a bribe against the headwind.

Wingspan

2.8 m on the great blue; 1.8 m of that is negotiable with the wind.

Gearing

Single speed. Gular sac doubles as a headwind sail, adding 4 mph downhill.

Call

Nominally silent. Under duress, and only on bicycles: honk.

Diet

Fish, 1 kg daily. Pannier capacity: one. Do not ask about the second.
