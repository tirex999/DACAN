Field guide

Read the bird.

Five things worth noticing while it coasts. Hover a note to light the plate.

  1. 1

    Cycling cap

    Six panels, worn over the nictitating membrane. Purely decorative, which is to say: essential.

  2. 2

    Gular pouch

    A stretch sack under the lower mandible, roughly 11 litres. It fishes with the beak as a scoop-net, then drains the water before the swallow.

  3. 3

    Wing

    Hollow bones, up to 2.5 m of span, and a thermal-lover's patience. On the bike it functions mainly as an arm and a sail.

  4. 4

    Fully webbed feet

    All four toes connected by webbing — the only birds with a complete web. Pedalling efficiency: surprising. Toe-wiggling: impossible.

  5. 5

    The machine

    Single-speed, drop bars, one bell, zero brakes. Gears were judged unnecessary by a bird that already owns the wind.
