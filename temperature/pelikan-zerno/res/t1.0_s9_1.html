Field notes · plate XIV

Four observations on an avian commuter

01

The pouch is not a basket.

It holds upward of 11 litres — a volume larger than the pelican's own stomach — and it exists purely as a fishing net, dragged sideways through water like a dip net on a very expensive handle. The milk crate bolted behind the saddle is, aerodynamically and legally, the bird's own idea.

gular pouchno storage rack fittedcrate is cargo
02

They fly in an echelon, not a V.

White pelicans ride the updraft off a coastline in a stepped diagonal line, each bird sitting in the lift generated by the wing ahead. Cyclists call the same thing a paceline. The pelican has simply refused to dismount for it.
