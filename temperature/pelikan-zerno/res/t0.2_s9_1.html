Notes

A brown pelican, adult, in a coral cycling cap, was observed riding a steel touring frame along the pier rail at dawn. Balance: unbothered. Pouch: occupied. The subject declined to comment and rang the handlebar bell twice.

  1. 06:12

    Arrival

    Coast road, 14 km/h, no hands on the descents. The bird free-wheeled past the bait cart without slowing.

  2. 06:26

    Mechanics

    Tarsometatarsus drives the crank; the visible joint bends backwards, which is exactly how a bird pedal-pumps. Webbed feet stay level on the pedals through the whole stroke.

  3. 06:41

    Load

    Gular pouch carrying one (1) mackerel, unstated purpose. Pannier contains a map, a shell, and a small grudge against the gulls.

  4. 07:03

    Departure

    Accelerated hard off the end of the pier, converted the last pedal stroke into a take-off, and left the bicycle leaning on the railing. It is still there.
