Field plate nº VII · coastal survey

PELICAN riding a BICYCLE

A survey of the common coastal commuter: seven kilograms of hollow bone, eleven litres of throat, one gear, and a cadence that never quite resolves into sense. Drawn at speed, from the dune path, at dusk.

observe

Field notes

01The approach

Does not ride to arrive. Rides to be seen arriving, usually against a low sun, usually with a wing on the handlebar.

02The pouch

Measured at 11.3 litres. Used for mackerel outbound and, on the return leg, for groceries and one small hat.

03The cadence

Both feet drive the same crank. Nobody has successfully explained this to a mechanic; the mechanic has stopped asking.

04Hazards

Loves boardwalks, tide lines, open crates. Startles at dogs, at seagulls, and at its own reflection in a car window.
