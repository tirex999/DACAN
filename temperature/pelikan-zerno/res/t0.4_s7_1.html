Plate II — Field manual

Six mechanisms of a pelican in motion

Hover a component to light it up on the technical drawing. Every part does double duty: flight gear that also happens to be cycling gear. The club has never managed to separate the two.

Drawn to scale by the club’s surveyor, who rides a heron and is not to be trusted on measurements.

Drawing 2 of 4 · gular pouch shown loaded

  1. 01 Gular cargo pouch

    11 litres, self-draining, fish-proof. Loads in one gulp and settles low for cornering.

  2. 02 Neck spring

    A living shock absorber. Soaks the sea-wall cobbles that would shake a heron to pieces.

  3. 03 Wing grip

    Drops, no bar tape. Feathers give better purchase in salt spray than any cork wrap.

  4. 04 Tail rudder

    Manual gearbox with three positions: up, down, and “bracing a crosswind off the point”.

  5. 05 Webbed drive feet

    34 cm² of pedal contact. Doubles as propulsion the moment the chain parts.

  6. 06 Brass bell

    One bell. Loud fish. Ring it on the blind dune crest, always, without exception.
