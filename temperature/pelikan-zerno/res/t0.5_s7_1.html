The pouch is the pannier

A gular pouch of working volume, loaded to the brim with the day's catch and one sand crab he refuses to name. Ride quality degrades noticeably above 2 kg.

Goggles, not glasses

Aviator optics, UV-4. Pelicans see a gull at 400 m and a sardine at 40 cm; on the boardwalk, mostly the gull.

03

Webbed, therefore slow

Four-toe pedals, no cleats, no shoes, no regrets. The trailing leg folds like a deckchair at the top of the stroke — biomechanically rude, visually excellent.

04

Why a pelican? Why a bicycle?

Because a fish, a bicycle and a bird of nearly three metres' wingspan have never once been asked to share a frame — and because the request, once made, is impossible to decline.

Road notes

One gear, chosen by the sea. Tail acts as fender; beak acts as basket, bumper and legal defence. Brakes were considered, then the tide came in, and the subject declined to comment. Observed descending at dusk, unbothered, fully laden, and approximately three fish ahead of schedule.
