AnomalyBirds require a headwind to soar. The subject generates its own, at will, by pedalling. Reviewers requested we stop calling it “cheating.”

01

Sail

The jaw is held two centimetres open. At speed the pouch inflates into a drogue that steadies the whole rig — the pilot insists this is aerodynamics and not appetite.

02

Drive

Webbed feet on platform pedals, knees flaring like a pair of opened scissors. Two legs, one crank, endless leverage over the animals on the embankment.

03

Warning

A chrome bell, mounted left, struck with the wing tip. Nothing on this road outruns a pelican that has decided to announce itself.
