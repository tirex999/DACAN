Why the pelican beats the bicycle

Give a pelican a bicycle and it will not, as the joke goes, beat a man. It will out-glide him. Pelecanus converts a two-knot sea breeze into hours of airborne travel with roughly one flap per thermal — a drivetrain efficiency that the humble chainring has never matched.

  1. 01

    The pouch is a net, not a pantry

    The gular pouch holds up to about 11 litres of water and fish — more than the bird's own stomach — and is emptied upward, like a shopping bag tipped into a mouth, within seconds of the scoop.

  2. 02

    Heaviest thing that flies, lightly

    The American white pelican reaches a wingspan near 2.5 m and a mass around 7 kg, at the very upper limit of what a bird can launch. Takeoff is a paddled, heroic run across the water.

  3. 03

    They draft like cyclists

    In V-formation, each bird rides the upwash of the one ahead and the flock rotates the lead position — the same trick a peloton uses, discovered some 30 million years earlier.

  4. 04

    Air-braked, head-first

    Brown pelicans fold in, tuck and drop from 10–20 m, hitting the water at speed and righting themselves with an inflatable throat sac that acts as a shock absorber. Bell not included.
