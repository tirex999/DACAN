Field notes

Observations logged from the railing, at a respectful, fish-free distance.

01 / Anatomy of a commute

The pouch is a net, not a bucket.

Distended it holds three times the volume of the bird's own gut, which is why the catch rides loose and gill-flapping through the whole commute. Steering is done with the wings; braking is done with dignity.

03 / Behaviour

He waves. Constantly.

Every passing heron receives a full wing-spread. Aerodynamically this is a disaster. Socially, it is a triumph.

Give me a lever long enough, a bicycle, and one webbed foot, and I shall ride the whole coastline. — the pelican, approximately
