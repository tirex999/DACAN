Field notes

Three observations from the saddle

Note 01

The pouch is not a helmet

It is a net, a sail and a rumen. Under load at 20 km/h it swings about 6 mm and the bird steers with it like a rudder — which is why the head stays so steady while everything below it bobs.

Note 02

Torque from a keel

Legs drive from the hip, not the knee. Short cranks, high cadence, and the whole body acts as a flywheel.

Note 03

One wing on the bar

Balance is a bargain: the far wing works, the near wing steers. Free a feather and the front wheel wanders within four metres.
