// two-bone solver, run 60× a second
const L1=80, L2=78, CR=30;
function knee(H,F,s){
  let d=F-H, θ=Math.atan2(d.y,d.x),
      L=clamp(dist(H,F),1,L1+L2-.01),
      a=Math.acos((L1*L1+L*L-L2*L2)/(2*L1*L));
  return{x:H.x+L1*Math.cos(θ+s*a),
         y:H.y+L1*Math.sin(θ+s*a)};
}

Method

One SVG <defs> rig, drawn once and re‑used at four scales — the main plate, two magnifications and a specimen diagram all share the same geometry.

Motion

Cranks drive the pedals; the pedals drive the ankles; the knees fall out of the law of cosines. Ground, boardwalk and dunes scroll at 1.25 / 1.0 / 0.7 / 0.6 parallax.
