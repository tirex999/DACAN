    Field notes

    Pelecanus occidentalis · boardwalk, evening flood tide · obs. no. 7

    The beak is not ballast — it is an airbrake, a fishing net and, on at least two recorded occasions, a bicycle bell. Note how the near wing never lets go of the bars, and how the feet rotate with the cranks: this is a bird that has accepted its mechanical fate.

    • 01Wings folded to a frontal area of 0.71 m² — a pelican in a crosswind is a sail with opinions.
    • 02Webbed feet clamp the pedals. No toe clips required, ever.
    • 03Pouch carries up to 11 litres of fish, i.e. eleven kilos of cargo without a pannier.
    • 04Bone density low, ego correspondingly high.
