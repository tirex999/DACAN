Create Tidehaven, a beautiful interactive 3D diorama of a small coastal island using Three.js + WebGL. The entire project must run in a browser from a single index.html file. Load libraries through a CDN and create all objects procedurally, without external 3D models.
VISUAL STYLE
A cozy miniature world with an isometric camera, soft shadows, expressive materials, and a warm natural palette. Deliver a polished original design with thoughtful composition, detailed scenery, smooth animations, and atmospheric lighting.
ISLAND AND CABIN
A small island with a sandy shore, rocks, grass, and trees.
A cozy A-frame cabin with a copper roof, wooden deck, and glowing windows.
A wooden pier with a ladder into the water, lanterns, and a moored kayak.
Lived-in details: a bench, towels, crates, and plants around the cabin.
CUTAWAY WATER - THE MAIN VISUAL FEATURE
Place the island inside a dimensional diorama containing a transparent turquoise lagoon.
The front and side faces must reveal the water in cross-section, like aquarium walls.
Keep the water surface, underwater animals, plants, and sandy seabed visible at the same time.
Add waves, shoreline foam, animated caustics on the seabed, and bubbles.
Avoid transparency sorting issues, flickering surfaces, and harsh intersections between the water and shore.
UNDERWATER LIFE
Several rays with smoothly undulating fins and tails.
Schools of small fish, aquatic plants, and rocks.
Animals should move naturally and remain inside the lagoon.
A button makes one ray leap out of the water, turn, and splash back down, creating droplets and expanding ripples.
INTERACTIONS
Launch kayak: sends the kayak on a trip around the lagoon. Pressing again returns it to the pier.
Ray leap: triggers the ray’s jump.
Tide: a slider smoothly changes the water level. Floating objects and surface effects must follow it.
Wind: adjusts wave strength and the movement of trees and plants.
Time of day: daytime, sunset, and night with smooth lighting transitions. Lanterns and cabin windows illuminate in the evening.
Pause: pauses the world’s animations.
Reset: restores the initial state.
CAMERAS
An overall isometric view of the island.
A cozy view near the cabin and pier.
An underwater view featuring rays and fish.
Free orbit using OrbitControls. Use smooth transitions between camera presets.
AUDIO
Add original, calm procedural music using the Web Audio API, along with water sounds and a gentle breeze. Include a music on/off button and a volume slider. Start audio only after a user interaction.
INTERFACE
Use English for all labels. Include the title Tidehaven, a short subtitle, compact translucent panels, refined typography, clear buttons, and visible active states. Keep controls away from the main scene. Make the control panels collapsible on mobile.
TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
Fully working interactions, without placeholders.
Delta-time-based animations.
Correct window resizing and responsive mobile support.
A capped pixel ratio and quality settings for less powerful devices.
Reuse geometry and materials, and use InstancedMesh for repeated details.
Organized, well-commented code.
Test every button, camera, audio control, water transparency, and the mobile layout.
Deliver a complete experience that invites exploration: a cozy island above and a living underwater world below.
